Where should we stay?

We're holding room blocks at the Courtyard by Marriott (Inner Harbor) and Delta Hotels Baltimore North under the Frede-Groff Wedding. Reserve by September 2, 2026 for the group rate.

What is the schedule for the day?

The ceremony begins at 3:00 PM, with the reception, dinner, and dancing to follow at 4:00 PM. The full run of the day is on the Schedule page.

Where is the venue?

The ceremony is at St. Ignatius Church (740 N. Calvert St) and the reception is at the Maryland Center for History & Culture (610 Park Ave), both in Mount Vernon and a short walk apart. See the Schedule for maps and directions.

Where can we park?

We'd point you to Chesapeake Center Parking (601 N Calvert St), a few blocks from the ceremony. The LAZ lot at the Walters Art Museum is closer, but you have to collect your keys by 7:00 PM, before the reception ends. Full details are on Travel & Stay.

How can we get there?

Baltimore/Washington International (BWI) is the nearest airport, about 20 minutes away, and Amtrak stops at Penn Station. The nearest Light RailLink stop is Mt. Vernon (Centre Street), and Lyft code LOVEGROVE is on us. Driving, parking, and transit details are all on Travel & Stay.

Have another question?

Reach out to us directly and we'll be happy to help. We'll keep adding answers here as more details come together.
